# Global Supply Chain Explorer — Nifty 50 & Nasdaq-100

An interactive D3.js force-directed supply chain network explorer covering **India's Nifty 50** and the **US Nasdaq-100**, including publicly listed suppliers, international partners, and unlisted/private companies.

## Features
- **3-Way Index Toggle:** Switch seamlessly between **Nifty 50** (`#nifty`), **Nasdaq-100** (`#nasdaq`), and **Combined** (`#combined`) cross-market supply chains without page reloads.
- **Listed vs. Unlisted Visibility:** Color-coded nodes and instant filters for **Listed** (India, US, International exchanges) and **Unlisted / Private** vendors, JVs, and subsidiaries.
- **Directional Supply Chain Inspector:** Click any company to inspect **Upstream Suppliers & Manufacturing**, **Subsidiaries & Joint Ventures**, **Parent / Holding Group**, **Cloud, Tech & Strategic Partners**, **Logistics & 3PL Providers**, and **Supplies To / Downstream Buyers**.
- **Rich Filtering & Controls:** Search across all index anchors and suppliers, filter by sector or relationship type, switch between Listing Status and Sector color modes, zoom/pan, and fit clusters to view.

## Live Hosting
Hosted as a static single-page application (`index.html`) compatible directly with **GitHub Pages** (no build step required).
